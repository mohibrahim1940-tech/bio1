FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Are there live teachers?

No. The academy is a self-study platform: PDFs, question banks, exams and mock tests you can use anytime. If you get a question wrong, our assistants explain it for you.

How do I get help with a mistake?

Open My Mistakes, tap Ask an assistant on any question, and send the message on WhatsApp. An assistant replies with an explanation, which is also added under the question in the academy.

How long is my account open?

For your plan's period (for example one month or three months). When it ends your account closes, and you can renew anytime from the login screen.

Is ACT Science only biology?

No — it mixes biology, chemistry, physics and earth science, but biology passages are very common. Our ACT units train the data skills that work for all of them.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Note that your account and scores are saved on the device you register on, so use the same phone or browser each time.

How do I pay?

Send your request on WhatsApp and we'll share the payment options.
